AI consulting

AI consulting for businesses.

AOA Global Services helps organizations adopt artificial intelligence where it produces a concrete result: more efficient processes, better-structured information and better-supported decisions.

Our AI consulting starts from the business, not the technology. We assess the operation, identify the use cases with real return, and design and implement the solution integrated with existing systems.

AI when it improves a process — not because it is fashionable.

We do not apply artificial intelligence because it is in fashion. We use it when it concretely improves a process, a decision or a business capability. In many cases the best solution combines automation, data integration and only the AI component that genuinely adds value.

What is included

AI consulting and implementation services.

  • AI opportunity assessment

    Review of processes, available information and systems to identify where artificial intelligence delivers a measurable improvement.

  • Solution design

    Definition of the use case, required data, architecture, risks and success criteria before any development.

  • AI-powered automation

    Workflows that combine rules, integrations and language models to reduce manual work.

  • AI agents and assistants

    Assistants connected to the organization’s information for customer service, internal support, request handling or operational queries.

  • Intelligent document processing

    Extraction, classification and validation of information from invoices, contracts, forms and emails.

  • Integration with existing systems

    Connection with ERP, CRM, databases and internal tools so AI operates inside the real process, not in isolation.

Use cases

Where AI usually adds value in an organization.

Use cases are prioritized by operational impact and feasibility with the information available.

  • Customer service and request handling

    Automatic classification, assisted responses and routing to the right team.

  • Operations and back office

    Data capture, reconciliations, validations and document generation.

  • Sales and follow-up

    Opportunity qualification, interaction summaries and automated follow-up.

  • Information and reporting

    Natural-language queries over business data and automatically generated reports.

How we work

From opportunity to a solution in operation.

  1. 01

    Understand

    We assess the process, the available data and the expected outcome.

  2. 02

    Design

    We define the use case, the solution, the scope and the evaluation criteria.

  3. 03

    Implement

    We develop, integrate and validate the solution with the team that will use it.
